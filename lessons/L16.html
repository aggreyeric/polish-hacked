"How Do I Get To…?" — The Travel Question

You already know Gdzie jest…? (Where is…?). But for getting to a place by transport, the better question is Jak dojechać do…? — "How do I get to…?" This asks for the route, not just the location.

💡 Grammar Note

Jak dojechać do + Genitive case. The destination goes into the Genitive after do. Example: Jak dojechać do centrum? (How do I get to the center?), Jak dojechać do lotniska? (How do I get to the airport?)

Tell a Travel Story (Past Tense)

Put it all together with a past-tense travel story. Here's a model you can adapt — "I went to Kraków last weekend."

PolishEnglish
W weekend pojechałem do Krakowa.I went to Kraków on the weekend. (m)
Pojechałam pociągiem.I went by train. (f)
Podróż trwała dwie godziny.The journey took two hours.
Zwiedzałem Stare Miasto.I explored the Old Town. (m)
Było pięknie.It was beautiful.
Wracaliśmy wieczorem.We came back in the evening.
💡 Past Tense Reminder

Remember the -łem (male) / -łam (female) ending from Lesson 8? Here it is in action: pojechałem / pojechałam (I went), zwiedzałem / zwiedzałam (I explored). Same pattern, different verbs.

🎯 Speaking Drill
Give directions to your house from the nearest station or stop. Use at least 4 direction phrases (skręć w lewo, idź prosto, przejdź przez, skręć w prawo). Then tell a short travel story in past tense: where you went, how you traveled, how long it took, and one thing you saw. Use the model above as a template. Record yourself — aim for 60–90 seconds total.
