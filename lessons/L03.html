Numbers 1–20: The Foundation

Learn numbers 1–20 first. Once you know these, 21–99 follows a simple pattern: twenty-one = dwadzieścia jeden (twenty + one). The same compound pattern works all the way to 99.

Pattern

21–99 = tens word + units word. Example: dwadzieścia jeden (21), trzydzieści pięć (35), czterdzieści dwa (42). The tens (30, 40, 50…) sound similar to the units — trzy (3) → trzydzieści (30), cztery (4) → czterdzieści (40). See the pattern? Learn 1–20 and you basically know 1–99.

"I Would Like…" — Your Most Useful Phrase

This one phrase unlocks ordering anything in Poland — food, drinks, tickets, directions. Learn both forms: one for male speakers, one for female speakers.

The Polish Restaurant Script: Entrance to Paying

Here's the full sequence you'll use in every Polish restaurant or café. Memorize these lines in order.

StepPolishEnglish
1. EnterDzień dobry!Good day!
2. Ask for tablePoproszę stolik dla dwóch osób.A table for two, please.
3. OrderChciałbym kawę i herbatę.I'd like a coffee and a tea.
4. Ask priceIle kosztuje?How much does it cost?
5. Ask for billPoproszę rachunek.The bill, please.
6. Pay & leaveDziękuję! Do widzenia!Thank you! Goodbye!

Speaking Drill

Roleplay ordering 3 items at a café. Say out loud: 1) "Dzień dobry!" 2) "Chciałbym/Chciałabym kawę, herbatę i wodę." 3) "Ile kosztuje?" 4) "Poproszę rachunek." 5) "Dziękuję! Do widzenia!" Record yourself and listen back. The goal is fluency — saying it without hesitating.
