The Golden Question: Gdzie jest…?

Two words unlock the entire city: Gdzie jest — "Where is." Drop in any place and you can find it. This is the single most useful phrase for a visitor in Poland. Memorize it cold.

💡 Pronunciation

Gdzie jest = /ɡʑɛ jɛst/. The "dz" in Gdzie is a soft "dź" sound — tongue forward. Practice: gdzie, gdzie, gdzie.

Emergency Phrases

If something goes wrong, these phrases can save you. Memorize at least the first two.

💡 Survival Tip

If you remember nothing else from this lesson, remember Gdzie jest…? (Where is…?) and Potrzebuję pomocy (I need help). With those two phrases plus the place names above, you can navigate any situation in Poland.

🎯 Speaking Drill
Ask for directions to three places using Gdzie jest…?. Example: "Gdzie jest toaleta?" (Where is the toilet?), "Gdzie jest dworzec?" (Where is the station?), "Gdzie jest apteka?" (Where is the pharmacy?). Then practice understanding the answer: say each direction word aloud — w lewo, w prawo, prosto. Finally, roleplay a full exchange: ask where the station is, "hear" the answer (left, straight, near), and repeat it back.
