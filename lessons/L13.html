Shopping Phrases — W Sklepie

Polish English When to use
Szukam + genitive I'm looking for… When you enter and want something specific
Czy macie…? Do you have…? Asking if a product exists
Czy mogę przymierzyć? Can I try it on? Before buying clothes
Ile kosztuje? How much does it cost? When no price tag is visible
Za drogo Too expensive Negotiating or reacting to price
Taniej Cheaper Asking for a lower price or comparing
Wezmę to I'll take it Deciding to buy
Czy mogę zapłacić kartą? Can I pay by card? At the register
💡 Genitive in Quantities

Remember from Lesson 7 — negation flips to genitive. But genitive is also used for quantities: "I want [amount] of [thing]." The noun after the quantity goes into the genitive case. For example: chcę dwóch koszul (I want two shirts), chcę pary butów (I want a pair of shoes). You're already using genitive — you just didn't know it had a name.

🎯 Speaking Drill
Roleplay a clothing store purchase. Enter the store, tell the clerk what you're looking for ("Szukam czarnej kurtki"), ask for a size, request to try it on ("Czy mogę przymierzyć?"), ask the price ("Ile kosztuje?"), negotiate ("Za drogo, czy jest taniej?"), and decide ("Wezmę to"). Do the whole exchange aloud without reading — use at least 5 vocab words.
