The Good News About Past Tense

Here's something most Polish courses won't tell you: past tense is easier than present tense. In present tense each verb follows one of four conjugation patterns with different endings. In past tense, there's one ending pattern that applies to every single verb. One pattern. All verbs. That's it.

The past tense is built from the verb stem + -ł- + a personal ending. The endings are small, predictable, and they encode gender — meaning the verb changes depending on whether you are male or female.

Gender Agreement: The Past Tense Endings

In Polish, the past tense verb must agree with the subject's gender. If you're male, you use one set of endings. If you're female, another. This applies to "I" (first person) and "you" (second person). Third person has its own forms.

Person Male speaker Female speaker Example (m) Example (f)
I -łem -łam mówiłem mówiłam
You (singular) -łeś -łaś mówiłeś mówiłaś
He / She -ł -ła mówił mówiła
We (mixed / m group) -liśmy -łyśmy mówiliśmy mówiłyśmy
You (plural) -liście -łyście mówiliście mówiłyście
They (m persons) -li -ły mówili mówiły
💡 The Universal Key

If you're male: -łem = "I did", -łeś = "you did". If you're female: -łam = "I did", -łaś = "you did". That's 80% of the past tense you'll ever speak. For "he/she", it's just -ł / -ła. The verb stem stays the same — only the ending changes.

How to Form the Past Tense

Take the infinitive, drop the -ć ending, and add the past tense endings. Example: mówić (to speak) → drop -ć → mówi- → add endings: mówiłem (I spoke, m), mówiłam (I spoke, f).

Some verbs lose a vowel or shift consonants in the stem (e.g. jeść → jadłem, iść → szedłem). You'll learn these irregulars naturally because they're the most common verbs — you'll use them every day.

Talking About Yesterday

The formula for telling stories about what you did is simple: Subject + past tense verb. Add time expressions (wczoraj = yesterday, rano = in the morning, wieczorem = in the evening) to create context.

💡 Example mini-story (male speaker)

Wczoraj rano poszedłem do pracy. Pracowałem cały dzień. Potem poszedłem do sklepu i kupiłem chleb. Wieczorem oglądałem film i poszedłem spać. — Yesterday morning I went to work. I worked all day. Then I went to the shop and bought bread. In the evening I watched a film and went to sleep.

💡 Same story (female speaker)

Wczoraj rano poszłam do pracy. Pracowałam cały dzień. Potem poszłam do sklepu i kupiłam chleb. Wieczorem oglądałam film i poszłam spać.

🎯 Speaking Drill
Tell a 2-minute story about what you did yesterday. Use at least 8 different past tense verbs. Include: where you went, what you ate, what you bought, who you talked to, and when you went to sleep. Say it aloud — don't write it first. Use your gender form (-łem if male, -łam if female).
