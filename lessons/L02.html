The Greetings Hierarchy: Formal vs. Informal

Polish has a strict formality system. Getting the greeting right is crucial — it sets the tone for the entire interaction. Use formal greetings with strangers, older people, and in professional settings. Use informal greetings only with friends, family, and people your own age who you already know.

The Verb "To Be" (być) — Your Key to 50 Sentences

This is the only verb you need to start speaking Polish. With these six forms, you can introduce yourself, describe people, state facts, ask questions, and build dozens of sentences. Memorize these six forms — they unlock everything.

PolishIPAEnglish
jestem/jɛstɛm/I am
jesteś/jɛstɛʂ/you are (singular, informal)
jest/jɛst/he/she/it is
jesteśmy/jɛstɛmɨ/we are
jesteście/jɛstɛɕtɕɛ/you are (plural, informal)
są/sɔ̃/they are
Pattern

Jestem + [noun/adjective] = "I am [noun/adjective]." That's it. Thirty combinations right there: Jestem studentem (I am a student), Jestem Amerykaninem (I am an American), Jestem zmęczony (I am tired).

Introducing Yourself: Name, Origin, Profession

Three sentences make a complete self-introduction. Learn these as fixed chunks — don't analyze the grammar yet. Just say them.

Nazywam się…
/nazɨvam ʂɛ/
My name is…
Jestem z…
/jɛstɛm z/
I am from… (e.g. Jestem z Ameryki)
Jestem…
/jɛstɛm/
I am… (e.g. Jestem studentem)
Mam na imię…
/mam na imʲɛ/
My first name is…
To jest…
/tɔ jɛst/
This is… (introducing someone else)
Miło mi
/mʲiwɔ mi/
Nice to meet you

The 5 Question Words That Open Every Door

Five words turn statements into questions: kto (who), co (what), gdzie (where), jak (how), dlaczego (why). Put them at the start of any sentence.

Kto to jest?
/kto tɔ jɛst/
Who is that?
Co to jest?
/tso tɔ jɛst/
What is that?
Gdzie jesteś?
/ɡʑɛ jɛstɛʂ/
Where are you?
Jak się masz?
/jak ʂɛ maʂ/
How are you?
Dlaczego?
/dlatʂɛɡɔ/
Why?

Speaking Drill

Record a 1-minute self-introduction. Say: your name (Nazywam się…), where you're from (Jestem z…), what you do (Jestem…), and ask the listener one question (Kto to jest? Co to jest? Gdzie jesteś?). Speak out loud — don't write it. Say it.
